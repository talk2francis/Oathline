## Capture takes

### Take A — mandate to deny

Record 35–45 seconds continuously: homepage boundary, `/mandate`, `/replay`, ruling. Do not rush the pointer. Capture the actual pages at 2560×1440, with UI zoom chosen for legibility before recording.

### Take B — state to execution

Record 30–40 seconds continuously: stale receipt, state refresh in the receipt timeline, inside-mandate ruling, real execution details. This is historical evidence presentation, not a reenacted live trade.

### Take C — verify to reconcile

Record 25–35 seconds continuously: load the shipped chain in `/verify`, then `/receipts/demo`, then `/surface`. Hold every result long enough to read before editing.

## Editorial rules

- Use cuts on causal events, not decorative transitions.
- Camera moves are 2–6% crops over 2–4 seconds; never whip-pan across UI.
- Keep the real pointer in interaction shots and remove it from static evidence holds.
- Use one visual highlight language: thin brass rule, soft cream mask, no glow.
- Never animate text as though it were emitted by the product when it was not.
- If an artifact is reformatted for readability, label the frame `FROM OBSERVED RECEIPT` and preserve its exact values.
