# Slow-drift scenario

A routine strategy proposes twelve BNBUSDT market buys of 5 USDT each over a session. Every individual order is below the 15 USDT per-order ceiling. Collectively they total 60 USDT and twelve orders, exceeding both the 40 USDT daily-gross limit and the three-orders-per-day limit.

This is a labeled simulated scenario. It does not execute any order.
